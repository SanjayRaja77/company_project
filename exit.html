Exit Policy Guidelines

1. Notice Period

Employees are required to serve a notice period of 60 days (2 months) from the date of resignation submission. Early relief is subject to management discretion and buyout options.

2. Handover Process

A complete Knowledge Transfer (KT) plan must be signed off by the Reporting Manager at least 5 days prior to the last working day.

3. Asset Return

All company assets (Laptops, ID Cards, Access Keys) must be returned to the IT/Admin department on the last working day.

4. Full & Final Settlement

F&F settlement will be processed within 45 days from the last working day, subject to clearance from all departments.
